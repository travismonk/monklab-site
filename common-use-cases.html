Why Byblos

Common Use Cases

Planning a Vacation

Current Workflow

Most people start by opening a collection of Airbnb listings. Interesting listings get sent into WhatsApp, Signal, Discord, or a family group chat. Notes about the listings live in the conversation. Ratings and comparisons are often kept in memory, a spreadsheet, or a separate notes application.

A week later, the listings still exist and the discussion still exists, but the relationship between them becomes harder to reconstruct. Which listing did everyone like? Which one was rejected? Which one was the cheapest? Which one had the pool?

Byblos

The listings, ratings, discussions, measurements, and decisions all live in the same workspace. Listings can be rated, discussed, tagged, measured, sorted, and filtered directly. The discussion stays attached to the listing itself.

Instead of sharing links and discussing them elsewhere, the listing becomes the place where the discussion happens.

Comparing Products

Current Workflow

Product comparisons often start as browser tabs and eventually turn into spreadsheets. One tab contains the product. Another contains reviews. Notes are written somewhere else. Costs, features, and comparisons get copied into a document or spreadsheet.

The spreadsheet gradually becomes the real source of truth while the browser becomes a collection of sources.

Byblos

The pages themselves become the records. Ratings, notes, prices, reactions, and measurements remain attached to the products they describe. Sort and filter can organize the pages directly without requiring a separate spreadsheet to become the center of the process.

The comparison stays connected to the products being compared.

Research

Current Workflow

Research usually accumulates across many systems. Browser tabs contain sources. Notes live in Obsidian, Notion, or a document. Discussions happen elsewhere. Bookmarks preserve links. Eventually the information survives, but the investigation itself becomes harder to reconstruct.

Weeks or months later, finding the source material is often easier than remembering the reasoning process.

Byblos

The tree preserves the investigation. Notes remain attached to sources. Discussions remain attached to pages. The path that led to a conclusion is preserved alongside the conclusion itself.

Instead of preserving only the material, Byblos preserves the process of understanding the material.

Collaborative Investigation

Current Workflow

A team investigating a problem often ends up using a browser, chat application, documents, spreadsheets, and task managers simultaneously. The evidence lives in webpages. The discussion lives in chat. Conclusions live in documents. Follow-ups live somewhere else.

Every tool is useful, but none of them understand the complete picture.

Byblos

The evidence becomes the primary object. Pages can accumulate discussion, notes, reactions, measurements, reminders, and decisions directly. The workspace can then reason about those relationships because they are part of the same system.

Instead of reconstructing an investigation from several tools, the investigation remains attached to the evidence that generated it.

Recurring Work

Current Workflow

Many people revisit the same collection of pages every day. Market data, sales leads, job listings, inventory systems, competitors, research sources, or project dashboards are checked repeatedly. Browser bookmarks help reopen the pages, but the context surrounding those visits often lives elsewhere.

People end up maintaining a collection of bookmarks, reminders, notes, and spreadsheets just to remember what changed.

Byblos

The pages remain in a workspace that accumulates context over time. Notes, discussions, measurements, and trackers stay attached to the pages they describe. The Inbox can surface pages that require attention, while the tree preserves the history of previous work.

The result is not simply a list of pages to revisit. It becomes a record of how those pages changed and what happened when they were last reviewed.

The Common Thread

Current workflow: browser plus other tools.

Byblos workflow: browser becomes the workspace.

That is the real value proposition that ties all five examples together.
